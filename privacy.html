Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

What you enter

To create your chart you enter a name or nickname, gender (female or male), birth date, birth time (optional) and birthplace. Gender is needed because saju reads it to set the direction of your 10-year luck cycles (daeun) and which stars stand for a partner in love readings; it is used for nothing else. This information is stored only on your device. We do not receive it on our servers.

Why we use it

Only to calculate your Four Pillars chart and to show your readings, fortunes and compatibility results inside the App.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, IP address and usage data to show and measure ads. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings. See Google’s privacy policy for details. We do not share your birth details with advertisers. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and some US states), the App asks for your consent or lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information before personalized ads are shown; you can change this choice at any time under My account > Ad privacy choices.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. We do not see your card details.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the App or in your device settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the App creates an image or message and hands it to the app you choose (for example Instagram). What happens next is governed by that app’s policy.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 13. If the birth date entered shows a user is under 13, the App does not save it. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will help remove any data.

Your choices

You can edit your details at any time, or delete them with “Delete my info” on the My account screen. Uninstalling the App also removes them from your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because your details stay on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. For anything else, contact minggoo147@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

We will post updates here and ask you to review material changes in the App. Contact: BokPal, Republic of Korea, minggoo147@gmail.com.
